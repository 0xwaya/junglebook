# Pandemonium

## Welcome to the revolutionary world of Pandemonium! 

Pandemonium is a unique web3 ecosystem that focuses on protecting the rare and majestic Macaws of our planet. At the heart of our ecosystem is our NFT collection of 4200 Pandemonium Macaws, each with a unique story and identity. Alongside the collection is our lottery smart contract integrated with the Chainlink VRF, ensuring that each week's Jungle Madness Lottery winner is selected transparently and fairly. 

We've incentivized early adoption of our SQUAWK NFTs with our Tree Nuts (NUTS) ERC20 reward token, whose value increases steadily over time. In addition, we donate a portion of our Pandemonium Macaw NFT sales to non-profit organizations dedicated to macaw conservation. 

To ensure the long-term value of the NFTs, we've implemented a scarcity-based tokenomics model. Early holders of our Pandemonium Macaw NFTs will have higher chances of winning a share of the NUTS prize pool in each Jungle Madness draw. 

Our roadmap is packed with exciting developments, including the initial drop and launch of the Jungle Madness lottery, our integration with unstoppable domains to enable wallets and domain utility, and partnerships with non-profit organizations. We're also excited about developing new lottery concepts and expanding our macaw conservation education efforts. 

Join us in this unique journey! At Pandemonium, we aim to revolutionize the NFT industry and save the macaws, one NFT at a time.
