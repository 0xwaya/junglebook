# Get Ready to Squawk 🦜

## Join Pandemonium's Wild Ride of Saving Macaws with NFTs and Lottery Fun! 🎉

Welcome to Pandemonium, the ultimate web3 ecosystem that combines the power of non-fungible tokens (NFTs), lottery smart contracts, Chainlink VRF, and ERC20 reward tokens. Pandemonium is created by a team of blockchain experts who are passionate about revolutionizing the NFT industry and saving the macaws.

Our NFT collection features 4200 unique and rare macaws, each with its own identity and story. The ERC721 contract is interfaced with a lottery smart contract that triggers on every NFT mint. To ensure fairness and randomness in the selection of a lottery winner, we have integrated Chainlink VRF. Our ecosystem also includes a reward ERC20 token, which is designed to incentivize holders and drive demand.

At Pandemonium, we are committed to creating value for our community. Our team has carefully crafted a scarcity-based tokenomics model that ensures value creation and stability. We believe that by building partnerships with non-profit organizations working on the preservation and protection of macaws in the wild, we can create a positive impact in the world.

Our roadmap for the next year includes an announcement of the initial drop, a spearmint raffle whitelist strategy, integration of NFTs with unstoppable domains to enable wallets and domain utility, as well as partnerships to enable a debit card that utilizes wallets connected to NFTs via unstoppable domains.

Join us on this adventure as we strive to revolutionize the NFT industry and create a positive impact for macaws and their habitat. Let's embark on this journey together and witness the magic of Pandemonium!
