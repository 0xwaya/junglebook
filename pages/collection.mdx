# Macaw NFT Collection

The Pandemonium collection is capped at **4,200 NFTs** and is designed as the identity layer for lottery eligibility, rewards alignment, and community participation.

## Collection Principles

- Transparent metadata and trait distribution policy
- Clear mint and ownership rules
- Eligibility mapping to lottery and reward mechanics

## Relationship to Lottery and Rewards

- NFT ownership is the qualification signal for lottery participation
- Reward logic must remain auditable and documented
- Any rarity or weighting rules must be published before activation
