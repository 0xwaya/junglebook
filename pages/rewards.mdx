# Tree Nuts (NUTS) Token

Jungle Madness Lottery rewards its winners with our Tree Nuts (NUTS) token. NUTS is an ERC20 token that is designed to create long-term value for our community and incentivize early adoption of our SQUAWK NFTs. 

## Scarcity-Based Tokenomics

We have implemented a scarcity-based tokenomics model to ensure NUTS token demand and value stability. The total supply of NUTS tokens is limited, and we have allocated a portion of the tokens to be distributed as rewards to winners in our Jungle Madness Lottery. 

Holding onto your SQUAWK NFTs earlier means you have a higher chance of receiving a share of the NUTS prize pool. With every weekly Jungle Madness draw, the prize pool increases as more SQUAWK NFTs are minted, adding to the scarcity of the NUTS token. 

## Benefits for NUTS Token Holders

Apart from the chance to win a share of the NUTS prize pool, holders of the NUTS tokens will have exclusive benefits, including governance rights and decision-making power over future developments of the Pandemonium ecosystem.

At Pandemonium, we are passionate about conservation, and we believe that everyone has a role to play in protecting our planet. To support macaw conservation, we are donating a portion of our sales from the Pandemonium Macaw NFT collection to non-profit organizations dedicated to macaw conservation.
